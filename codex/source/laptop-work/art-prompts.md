## 坐姿保留袜子

Local product sprite repair. Match the supplied transparent CROPPED FOOT DETAIL nearly pixel-for-pixel, with the same composition, cropped edges, existing dark indigo opaque sock, pale cushion and navy skirt. The ONE change is to remove the dark brown loafer and show the foot covered entirely in the same smooth dark navy-indigo knitted sock as the leg. The fabric must seamlessly continue over the ankle and rounded toe. Sock foot is only a few pixels narrower than shoe, same location, angle, and lighting. Dark purple-navy cloth, gentle toe contour, no straps, cuffs, seams, laces, soles, buckles or footwear, no bare skin. Keep skirt hem and cushion visually unchanged. Preserve alpha and keep original margin and framing. Output a transparent cropped detail, no additions.

## 坐姿停下思考

For this thinking animation all tapping stops. BOTH HANDS REMAIN RESTING ON THE EXACT SAME KEYBOARD KEYS in every cel, and pose, laptop, knees, table, ponytail behind the shoulder and camera composition stay exactly in position. The only movement is her HEAD: (1) looks quietly at the keyboard and screen as in reference, (2) raises her chin and moves gaze a little ahead, (3) slight attentive tilt and looks upward about ten degrees, (4) stays looking up, eyes briefly narrowed in thought, (5) lowers chin a little with soft open pupils, (6) relaxes back toward the reference pose. All motion is restrained, subtle, lively and anatomically coherent. Do not raise a hand or add bubbles. Her feet remain fully covered by dark navy socks, with no shoes. Same scale and ground line, plenty of transparent gutters, no text or floating icons. Age-appropriate everyday outfit.
